## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between Azure Gold Partner and Azure Expert MSP?

**Azure Gold Partner** demonstrates competency in specific Azure solution areas, while **Azure Expert MSP** is the highest tier of managed service provider designation, requiring extensive operational excellence and customer success metrics.

### How long does Azure migration typically take?

Migration timelines vary based on complexity:
- **Small applications**: 2-4 weeks
- **Medium enterprises**: 2-4 months
- **Large enterprises**: 6-12 months
The phased approach allows for continuous business operations during migration.

### What are the costs associated with Azure migration?

Costs include:
- Assessment and planning
- Migration execution
- Azure infrastructure costs
- Training and change management
- Ongoing managed services

Partners like TechResona provide detailed cost estimates and ROI analysis upfront.

### Is Azure compliant with Indian data protection regulations?

Yes, Azure India regions comply with:
- Information Technology Act, 2000
- RBI guidelines for financial services
- IRDAI regulations for insurance
- Data localization requirements
- ISO 27001, SOC 2, and other certifications

### Can I migrate only part of my infrastructure to Azure?

Absolutely! **Hybrid cloud** approaches are common, allowing you to:
- Keep certain workloads on-premises
- Migrate others to Azure
- Maintain connectivity through Azure Arc
- Gradually transition at your own pace
