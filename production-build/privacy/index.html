Your Data Protection Rights

You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.

Right to Rectification

Right to Erasure

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.

Right to Restrict Processing

You have the right to request restriction of processing your personal data under certain conditions.

Right to Data Portability

You have the right to request transfer of your data to another organization or directly to you.

Right to Object

You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data for marketing purposes.

Right to Withdraw Consent

You have the right to withdraw consent at any time where we relied on consent to process your data.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@techresona.com. We will respond within 30 days.
